NORTHWIND LABS CUSTOMER REFUND POLICY

Standard Refunds
Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase for any reason.
Refunds are processed to the original payment method and typically appear within
5 to 7 business days.

Annual Subscriptions
Annual subscriptions are eligible for a prorated refund if cancelled after the
initial 30 day window. The prorated amount is calculated from the cancellation
date through the end of the paid term, minus a 10 percent administrative fee.

Non-Refundable Items
Setup fees, professional services engagements, and custom integration work are
non-refundable once work has begun. Training sessions are refundable only if
cancelled at least 5 business days before the scheduled date.

Enterprise Contracts
Enterprise customers with contracts above 50,000 dollars annually are governed
by the refund terms in their individual master service agreement, which
supersede this policy.

Disputes
Refund disputes should be escalated to billing-support@northwindlabs.example.
Unresolved disputes after 14 days are reviewed by the Director of Customer
Success.
