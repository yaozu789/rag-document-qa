NORTHWIND LABS EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

Office Hours
The main office is open Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM Pacific Time.
The office is closed on weekends and all company holidays. Badge access outside
these hours requires written approval from a department head.

Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements
require approval from both your manager and the People Operations team. All
remote employees must be reachable during core hours, which are 10:00 AM to
3:00 PM Pacific Time.

Equipment
Every new employee receives a laptop, an external monitor, and a headset. Laptop
refresh happens every three years. Damaged equipment should be reported to
it-help@northwindlabs.example within 48 hours of the incident.

Paid Time Off
Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid vacation per year during their first
two years, increasing to 20 days per year starting in year three. Unused
vacation carries over up to a maximum of 10 days. Sick leave is separate and
accrues at 10 days per year with no cap on carryover.

Parking
The garage on Fulton Street offers 40 reserved spots on a first-come basis.
Monthly parking passes cost 85 dollars and are payroll deductible.
